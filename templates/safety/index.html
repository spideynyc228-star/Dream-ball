DREAM BALL SAFETY

A celebration where everyone feels respected.

These shared commitments help keep the school event welcoming, private and enjoyable for every student.

01

Community rules

Partnership requests should be kind and pressure-free. A “no” or no response must always be accepted gracefully.

02

Safe communication

Keep communication focused on the school event. Never pressure someone to share personal information.

03

Meeting safety

Plan rehearsals in familiar, appropriate locations and let a parent, guardian or trusted school adult know your plans.

04

Privacy & reporting

Do not repost a classmate’s profile or photo. Report an inappropriate interaction or speak with a teacher early.

ONLINE SAFETY CHECKLIST

Before the event

  • Write only respectful, pressure-free partnership requests.
  • Choose familiar, appropriate rehearsal locations.
  • Keep private profile information private.
  • Confirm your arrival and return-home plan.
  • Contact a trusted adult or school staff member if you need help.
