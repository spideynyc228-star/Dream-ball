WHY DREAM BALL EXISTS

A beautiful event begins with a community that feels safe.

Big school celebrations should be easy to look forward to. Dream Ball brings invitations, profiles, partnership requests and preparation into one calm, school-supported space.

Every participant joins through a personal invitation. Every profile is reviewed. The result is less uncertainty, more confidence and a clearer path to celebrating together.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Everything you need to know.

Is Dream Ball a dating app?+

No. Dream Ball is an official school-event coordination platform. It helps invited students explore dance partnerships in a moderated, respectful environment.

Who can join?+

Only students with a personal invitation code issued by their school can register.

What happens to my profile?+

Your profile is reviewed by the moderation team before other approved students can see it. You can update it any time; updates return it to review.

What if I need help?+

Use the report tool, speak with a trusted teacher, or review the safety guide. Support is always part of the platform.
